# 🔐 TASK 6: Research Report — The Importance of Patch Management

## 📌 Overview

This research report explains the importance of **Patch Management** in cybersecurity and how timely security updates help organizations protect systems from known vulnerabilities.

The report focuses on how vulnerabilities are discovered, how patches are released and deployed, and what can happen when organizations delay security updates.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* 🔍 Understand the importance of patch management
* 🛡️ Learn how patches protect systems from known vulnerabilities
* ⚠️ Understand the risks of unpatched software
* 📊 Study real-world incidents caused by delayed patching
* 🔄 Understand the patch management lifecycle
* ✅ Identify patch management best practices

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

* 🔹 **Patch:** A software update that fixes bugs or security vulnerabilities.
* 🔹 **Vulnerability:** A weakness in software that can be exploited by attackers.
* 🔹 **CVE:** A unique identifier assigned to publicly known vulnerabilities.
* 🔹 **CVSS:** A scoring system used to measure the severity of vulnerabilities.
* 🔹 **Patch Management:** The process of identifying, testing, deploying, and verifying software updates.

---

## 🔄 Patch Management Lifecycle

1. 🔎 **Discovery** – Identify missing patches and vulnerable systems.
2. 📋 **Assessment** – Evaluate vulnerability severity and business risk.
3. 🧪 **Testing** – Test patches before production deployment.
4. 🚀 **Deployment** – Apply patches to affected systems.
5. ✔️ **Verification** – Confirm that patches were successfully installed.

---

## 💥 Real-World Case Studies

### 🦠 WannaCry — 2017

* Exploited the **EternalBlue vulnerability (CVE-2017-0144)**.
* Microsoft had already released a security patch.
* More than **300,000 systems** were affected across **150+ countries**.
* Organizations including the NHS and automobile manufacturers were affected.
* Estimated global economic impact: **around $4 billion**.
* 📌 **Key Lesson:** Delaying an available security patch can allow malware to spread rapidly.

### 🏢 Equifax Breach — 2017

* Exploited **Apache Struts CVE-2017-5638**.
* A security patch was available before the attack.
* Attackers remained inside the network for more than **76 days**.
* Approximately **147 million people** were affected.
* The incident resulted in significant financial and legal consequences.
* 📌 **Key Lesson:** Critical vulnerabilities on public-facing systems must be patched quickly and verified.

---

## 📊 Important Findings

* ⚠️ Unpatched vulnerabilities are a major cause of successful cyber attacks.
* ⏱️ Delaying patches increases the exploitation window.
* 🧪 Testing helps prevent compatibility and operational problems.
* 🤖 Automation can reduce repetitive patching tasks.
* 🔍 Verification is necessary because a scheduled patch may not reach every endpoint.
* 🗂️ Maintaining an accurate asset inventory is essential for effective patch management.

---

## ✅ Seven-Step Patch Management Checklist

* 1️⃣ Inventory all systems and software
* 2️⃣ Establish a clear patching policy
* 3️⃣ Prioritize vulnerabilities based on risk
* 4️⃣ Automate patch discovery and deployment
* 5️⃣ Test patches before production
* 6️⃣ Document and report patch status
* 7️⃣ Monitor systems continuously

| 👥 Limited Resources      | Automate repetitive tasks              |
| ☁️ Complex Infrastructure | Use centralized patch management       |

---

## 🛡️ Best Practices

* 🔴 Prioritize critical vulnerabilities.
* ⏱️ Patch high-risk systems as quickly as possible.
* 🧪 Test updates before production deployment.
* 🤖 Automate patch discovery and deployment.
* 🔍 Verify successful installation.
* 📝 Maintain detailed patch records.
* 🖥️ Replace or isolate end-of-life systems.
* 🌐 Use network segmentation to reduce attack impact.
* 📈 Continuously monitor for new vulnerabilities.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaway

> **Patch management is a fundamental part of cybersecurity.**

Keeping software updated reduces the number of known weaknesses that attackers can exploit. The **WannaCry and Equifax incidents** demonstrate why organizations should identify, prioritize, deploy, and verify security patches without unnecessary delay.
